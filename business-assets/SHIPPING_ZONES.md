## Free Shipping Rules

1. **Threshold:** free standard shipping on orders of `<<PKR 5,000>>` or more, before shipping charges and after discounts.
2. Threshold is evaluated on the **post-discount subtotal**, not the pre-discount value.
3. Free shipping applies to **Zones 1–3** only. Zone 4 receives `<<PKR 150>>` off shipping instead of full waiver.
4. Free shipping does **not** apply to Zone 5 (international) at any order value.
5. Free shipping is not combinable with a shipping-specific coupon; the system applies the better of the two, never both.
6. If a partial return drops the order below the threshold, shipping is **not** retroactively charged.
7. Campaign-wide free shipping (e.g. Eid) overrides the threshold for its stated dates and must have an explicit end date.

## Delivery Attempt Policy

1. Courier attempts delivery up to `<<3>>` times.
2. After attempt 1 fails, the support team contacts the customer within `<<4>>` working hours.
3. After attempt 3, the parcel is returned to origin (RTO).
4. RTO parcels are restocked within `<<2>>` working days after QC inspection.
5. Prepaid RTO orders are refunded minus actual shipping cost, unless the failure was our error.

## Address Quality Rules

- Require: full name, complete street address, area/sector, city, province, postal code, active mobile number.
- Reject or query addresses shorter than `<<15>>` characters or lacking a city.
- Landmarks are encouraged for Zone 3–4 deliveries.
- One phone number must be reachable; a secondary number is recommended for Zone 4.
