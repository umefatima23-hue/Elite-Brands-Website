# Shipping Policy

_Last updated: 26 July 2026_

## Coverage

Elite Brands ships across Pakistan. International shipping is not currently available.

## Rates

| Order value        | Shipping fee |
| ------------------ | ------------ |
| Under Rs 5,000     | Rs 250       |
| Rs 5,000 and above | Free         |

Cash on delivery (COD) may include an additional handling fee of Rs 100, shown at checkout.

## Delivery Times

| Zone                                            | Working days |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi          | 2–4          |
| Other major cities (Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta) | 3–5   |
| Remaining cities and towns                      | 5–7          |

Working days exclude Sundays and public holidays. Orders placed after 15:00 PKT are processed the next working day.

## Dispatch

- Orders are dispatched from our Karachi warehouse.
- You will receive a tracking link by email and SMS once your order ships.
- Signature or CNIC verification may be required for high-value orders.

## Failed Deliveries

Couriers make up to two delivery attempts. If both fail, the order is returned to our warehouse and the customer is contacted to arrange redelivery (redelivery fees may apply) or a refund.

## Address Accuracy

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. Elite Brands is not liable for delays caused by incorrect or incomplete addresses.

## Order Changes

Address changes can be requested within 2 hours of placing the order, provided the order has not been dispatched. Email support@elitebrands.pk.

## Damaged or Missing Items

Report damage or missing items within 48 hours of delivery to support@elitebrands.pk with your order number and clear photos. See the Return Policy for next steps.
