# Return & Exchange Policy

_Last updated: 26 July 2026_

We want you to love every piece. If something isn't right, here's how returns and exchanges work.

## Return Window

Return requests must be raised within **7 days of delivery**.

## Eligible Items

- Unstitched fabric in original packaging, with tags intact.
- Unworn, unwashed ready-to-wear with tags attached and original packaging.

## Non-Returnable Items

- Sale and clearance items.
- Stitched, altered or customised pieces.
- Jewellery, intimate accessories and dupattas sold separately.
- Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page.

## Condition on Return

Items must be returned in the same condition they were received — unworn, unwashed, unaltered, free of stains, perfume and odours, with all tags and original packaging.

Elite Brands reserves the right to refuse returns that do not meet these conditions.

## How to Return

1. Email returns@elitebrands.pk within 7 days of delivery with your order number and reason for return.
2. Our team will confirm eligibility and share the return address within 2 working days.
3. Pack the item securely with the original packaging and tags.
4. Ship the item back via a trackable courier. Return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the item is faulty or incorrect.
5. Once received and inspected (within 3 working days), we'll process your refund or exchange.

## Refunds

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 working days of inspection.
- COD refunds are issued via bank transfer; please share account details when requested.
- Original shipping fees are non-refundable.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If your preferred size or piece is unavailable, we'll issue a refund instead.

## Faulty or Incorrect Items

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect item, contact support@elitebrands.pk within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos. We'll arrange a free return pickup and issue a full refund or replacement.
