# Fabric Guide

A reference for the fabrics you'll see across Elite Brands.

## Lawn
Lightweight cotton weave, soft against the skin, ideal for Pakistan's summer. Sold as printed lawn (everyday) and luxury lawn (embroidered, often with a cotton net dupatta).

**Best for:** Everyday summer wear, workwear, casual events.

## Cotton
Breathable, durable and easy to care for. Ranges from fine voile to heavier weaves.

**Best for:** Daily wear, kurtas, layering.

## Chiffon
Sheer, lightweight and flowy. Usually embroidered for formal use. Requires layering.

**Best for:** Formals, occasion wear, dupattas.

## Organza
Crisp, sheer and structured. Holds embellishment beautifully.

**Best for:** Formal shirts, dupattas, bridal accents.

## Silk
Rich, lustrous and cool to the touch. Ranges from raw silk to fine silk satin.

**Best for:** Formals, occasion wear, bridal.

## Cotton Net
Soft cotton mesh, popular as a dupatta base in luxury lawn collections.

**Best for:** Dupattas paired with embroidered lawn shirts.

## Net
Fine synthetic mesh. Holds heavy embroidery. Requires an inner lining.

**Best for:** Formal shirts, bridal, occasion dupattas.

## Karandi
Textured winter fabric — soft, matte and slightly heavy. A blend that reads warmer than lawn without the weight of wool.

**Best for:** Autumn and winter suits.

## Khaddar
Handwoven cotton weave with a rustic texture. Warmer than lawn, ideal for cooler months.

**Best for:** Winter unstitched suits.

## Wool
Warm, structured and long-lasting. Used in shawls and heavier winter pieces.

**Best for:** Winter shawls, formal winter wear.

## Cambric
Fine, tightly woven cotton with a smooth finish. Common in trouser fabric.

**Best for:** Trousers paired with lawn or cotton shirts.

## Jacquard
Woven fabric with a pattern integrated into the weave itself. More formal than a print.

**Best for:** Occasion wear, luxury unstitched.

## Embellishment Techniques

- **Chikankari:** Delicate white-on-white or tonal hand embroidery, originally from Lucknow.
- **Zardozi:** Metallic thread work, often with pearls and stones. Heavy, formal, occasion-only.
- **Aari:** Fine chain-stitch embroidery worked with a hook. Fluid and intricate.
- **Block Print:** Hand-carved wooden blocks stamped onto fabric. Distinctive slight irregularity is a mark of authenticity.
- **Digital Print:** Modern high-resolution printing. Sharper detail and colour range than traditional print.

## Choosing by Season

| Season      | Recommended                       |
| ----------- | --------------------------------- |
| Summer      | Lawn, cotton, cotton net, chiffon |
| Spring/Fall | Cotton, cambric, light karandi    |
| Winter      | Karandi, khaddar, wool            |
| Festive     | Chiffon, organza, silk, net       |
