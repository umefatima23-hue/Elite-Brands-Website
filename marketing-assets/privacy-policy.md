# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 26 July 2026_

Elite Brands ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights.

## Information We Collect

- **Account details:** name, email, phone number, password (hashed).
- **Order details:** billing and shipping address, items purchased, order value.
- **Payment details:** processed by our payment providers. We do not store full card numbers.
- **Usage data:** pages visited, referring URL, device and browser, IP address, session identifiers.
- **Communications:** messages sent to support, subscription preferences.

## How We Use Your Information

- To process and deliver orders.
- To provide customer support.
- To send transactional messages (order confirmations, shipping updates).
- To send marketing communications (only with your consent).
- To improve the website, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

## Legal Basis

We process personal information on the basis of: performance of a contract (orders), consent (marketing), legitimate interests (site improvement, fraud prevention), and legal obligation (tax and regulatory records).

## Sharing

We share personal information only with:

- Payment processors, to complete transactions.
- Courier partners, to deliver orders.
- Cloud infrastructure providers that host the site.
- Analytics providers, on an aggregated and pseudonymised basis where possible.
- Authorities, when required by law.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Cookies

We use cookies for authentication, cart persistence, analytics and (with consent) marketing. You can control cookies via your browser. Disabling essential cookies may break checkout.

## Data Retention

- Order and tax records: retained for the period required by law (typically 6 years).
- Account data: retained while your account is active; you can request deletion at any time.
- Marketing data: retained until you unsubscribe.

## Your Rights

You can request to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Delete your account and associated personal data (subject to legal retention).
- Withdraw consent for marketing at any time.
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@elitebrands.pk.

## Security

We use industry-standard measures — encryption in transit (TLS), hashed passwords, access controls and audit logging. No system is perfectly secure; report suspected issues to security@elitebrands.pk.

## Children

Elite Brands is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be highlighted on the homepage and, where required, notified by email.
