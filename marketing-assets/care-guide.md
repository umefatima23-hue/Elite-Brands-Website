# Care Guide

Designer fabrics deserve gentle handling. Follow these guidelines to keep every piece looking its best.

## General Rules

- Always read the care label sewn into the garment — it overrides this guide.
- Dry clean embellished, embroidered and formal pieces for the first wash.
- Wash dark and light colours separately.
- Never wring embroidered fabrics; press gently between towels.
- Store in a cool, dry wardrobe away from direct sunlight.

## By Fabric

### Lawn
- Cold hand wash or gentle machine cycle, inside out.
- Mild detergent, no bleach.
- Line dry in shade — direct sun fades prints.
- Iron on medium heat while slightly damp.

### Cotton
- Cold or warm wash, inside out.
- Mild detergent.
- Line dry in shade to preserve colour.
- Iron on medium-high heat.

### Chiffon & Organza
- Dry clean recommended.
- If hand washing: cold water, no rubbing, mild detergent.
- Never wring. Roll in a towel to remove excess water.
- Iron on low heat with a pressing cloth.

### Silk
- Dry clean only.
- Do not spray perfume directly onto silk.
- Store folded with acid-free tissue between layers.

### Cotton Net / Net
- Dry clean recommended.
- If home washing: cold hand wash, no rubbing.
- Air dry flat.
- Iron on low with a pressing cloth.

### Karandi & Khaddar
- Cold hand wash or gentle machine cycle, inside out.
- Mild detergent.
- Line dry in shade.
- Iron on medium-high heat.

### Wool
- Dry clean recommended.
- Store folded, not hung, to prevent stretching.
- Use cedar or lavender to deter moths.

## Embellishments

- Never machine wash pieces with pearl, sequin, zardozi or beadwork.
- Steam gently — do not press directly on embellished areas.
- Fold with acid-free tissue over the embellishment.
- Transport in the garment bag it arrived in.

## Stain Care

- Blot immediately with a clean cloth — do not rub.
- Do not apply water to silk or dry-clean-only fabrics; take to a professional cleaner.
- Mention the stain and fabric to your dry cleaner.

## Storage

- Wardrobes should be dry and ventilated.
- Hang pret and pressed suits on padded hangers.
- Fold embellished pieces flat with tissue.
- Refold silk and net pieces every few months to avoid creasing at the folds.

## Ironing Guide

| Fabric      | Heat   | Notes                        |
| ----------- | ------ | ---------------------------- |
| Lawn        | Medium | While slightly damp          |
| Cotton      | High   | Direct                       |
| Chiffon     | Low    | Pressing cloth               |
| Silk        | Low    | Pressing cloth, inside out   |
| Net         | Low    | Pressing cloth               |
| Karandi     | Medium | Direct                       |
| Wool        | Steam  | Do not press hard            |
