# Frequently Asked Questions

## Orders

**How do I place an order?**
Browse the collection, add pieces to your cart and check out. You'll receive an order confirmation by email within a few minutes.

**Can I modify or cancel my order?**
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement, provided they have not been dispatched. Email support@elitebrands.pk with your order number.

**Do I need an account to order?**
No. Guest checkout is available. Creating an account lets you track orders, save addresses and manage your wishlist.

## Payment

**What payment methods do you accept?**
Debit and credit cards, bank transfer, and cash on delivery (COD) within Pakistan. COD availability may depend on your city.

**Is my payment information secure?**
Yes. Payments are processed through PCI-compliant providers. Elite Brands never stores full card numbers.

**Which currency are prices in?**
All prices are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR).

## Shipping

**Where do you ship?**
We ship across Pakistan. International shipping is not yet available.

**How long does delivery take?**
- Major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi): 2–4 working days.
- Other cities: 4–7 working days.

**How much does shipping cost?**
Standard shipping is Rs 250. Orders over Rs 5,000 ship free.

**Can I track my order?**
Yes. You'll receive a tracking link by email and SMS once your order ships.

## Returns & Exchanges

**What is your return policy?**
Unstitched fabric and unworn ready-to-wear can be returned within 7 days of delivery, with original packaging and tags intact.

**Which items cannot be returned?**
Sale items, altered or stitched pieces, jewellery, and intimate accessories are non-returnable.

**How do I request a return?**
Email returns@elitebrands.pk with your order number and reason. See the full Return Policy for the process.

**When will I receive my refund?**
Refunds are issued within 7 working days of the returned item being received and inspected.

## Products

**Are products authentic?**
Yes. Every piece is sourced directly from the designer house or an authorised distributor.

**How do I know if something is in stock?**
Stock is shown on the product page. Out-of-stock items are marked and cannot be added to the cart.

**Do product colours match the photos?**
We photograph pieces in natural light and edit minimally, but slight variation is possible between screens.

**Do you offer stitching services?**
Not at this time. All ready-to-wear pieces arrive stitched; unstitched suits arrive as fabric.

## Care

**How should I care for my pieces?**
See our Care Guide for fabric-specific washing and storage instructions.
