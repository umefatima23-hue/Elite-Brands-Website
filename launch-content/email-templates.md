# Email Templates — Elite Brands

Sentence case in body, Title Case in subjects. Every email closes with brand signature and unsubscribe (except transactional).

## 1. Welcome Email

- **Subject:** Welcome to Elite Brands.
- **Preheader:** Pakistan's finest designers, one address.

```

Welcome to Elite Brands — the new home for Pakistan's most-loved designer labels.

From Sana Safinaz and Maria B to Khaadi, Sapphire and Elan, we've brought fifteen designer houses into one curated storefront. Every piece is 100% original, in stock, and ships free across Pakistan.

Start exploring:
• New Arrivals — {{new_arrivals_link}}
• Luxury Lawn — {{luxury_lawn_link}}
• The Eid Edit — {{eid_link}}

With love,
The Elite Brands Team
```
