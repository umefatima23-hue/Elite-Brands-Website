# Fabric Glossary — Elite Brands

A reference for merchandising, product entry, and customer education. Each entry lists the fabric's season, texture, best use, and care instructions.

---

## Lawn
- **Season:** Spring / Summer
- **Texture:** Lightweight, fine cotton weave with a soft, breathable hand.
- **Best use:** Everyday summer wear, printed and embroidered casual suits.
- **Care:** Cold machine wash, mild detergent, dry in shade, iron on medium.

## Cotton
- **Season:** Spring / Summer
- **Texture:** Medium-weight, breathable, slightly crisper than lawn.
- **Best use:** Daily wear pret, kids' clothing, uniforms.
- **Care:** Machine wash cold, tumble dry low, iron high while damp.

## Khaddar
- **Season:** Autumn / Winter
- **Texture:** Handwoven, coarse, matte finish with visible slubs.
- **Best use:** Winter unstitched suits, casual layering.
- **Care:** Hand wash cold first time to set colour, iron medium-high.

## Linen
- **Season:** Autumn / Winter transition
- **Texture:** Crisp, breathable, natural creasing.
- **Best use:** Semi-formal winter pret, structured shirts.
- **Care:** Dry clean recommended; if washing, cold gentle cycle, iron damp.

## Chiffon
- **Season:** All seasons; festive
- **Texture:** Sheer, lightweight, flowing with a slight grain.
- **Best use:** Dupattas, formal shirts, festive layering.
- **Care:** Hand wash cold or dry clean, no wringing, iron low with cloth.

## Organza
- **Season:** Festive / Bridal
- **Texture:** Sheer, crisp, holds structure.
- **Best use:** Embroidered dupattas, festive shirts, bridal accents.
- **Care:** Dry clean only, store flat or rolled, steam not iron.

## Silk
- **Season:** Autumn / Winter, festive
- **Texture:** Smooth, lustrous, drapes fluidly.
- **Best use:** Luxury pret, formal trousers, dupattas, blouses.
- **Care:** Dry clean only, avoid direct sunlight, store in breathable cover.

## Karandi
- **Season:** Autumn / Winter
- **Texture:** Blended weave (cotton + wool), soft with subtle warmth.
- **Best use:** Winter unstitched, everyday winter pret.
- **Care:** Dry clean preferred; hand wash cold if necessary, iron medium.

## Jacquard
- **Season:** Winter, festive
- **Texture:** Woven pattern, textured surface, medium-heavy weight.
- **Best use:** Formal shirts, festive suits, shawls.
- **Care:** Dry clean only, iron on reverse side, steam preferred.

## Cambric
- **Season:** Autumn / early Winter
- **Texture:** Tightly woven cotton, smooth, slightly heavier than lawn.
- **Best use:** Transition-season unstitched, printed casuals.
- **Care:** Machine wash cold, iron medium-high.

## Velvet
- **Season:** Winter, bridal
- **Texture:** Dense pile, plush, rich sheen.
- **Best use:** Bridal, festive shirts, shawls, jackets.
- **Care:** Dry clean only, steam on reverse side, never iron the pile.

## Net
- **Season:** Festive / Bridal
- **Texture:** Open mesh, lightweight, holds embroidery well.
- **Best use:** Bridal dupattas, festive shirts, embroidered overlays.
- **Care:** Dry clean only, store flat, do not fold sharply along embroidery.
