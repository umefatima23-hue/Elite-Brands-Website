# Product Badges — Elite Brands

Short, on-brand badge copy for product cards, PDPs, and campaign tiles. Keep to 1–2 words for card overlays; the longer variant is for banners or PDP callouts.

| Badge | Short (card) | Long (banner / PDP) | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New Arrival | New Arrival | Just In — Fresh from the Atelier | First 21 days after publish |
| Limited Stock | Limited Stock | Only a Few Pieces Left | Inventory < 10 units total |
| Best Seller | Best Seller | A House Favourite | Top 10% by units sold this season |
| Trending | Trending | Loved This Week | Top 20 by views in last 7 days |
| Premium | Premium | Premium Craftsmanship | Curated premium tier |
| Luxury | Luxury | The Luxury Edit | Luxury pret / bridal collections |
| Exclusive | Exclusive | Only at Elite Brands | Not sold elsewhere online |
| Sale | Sale | On Sale — Ends Soon | Any active markdown |
| Fast Selling | Selling Fast | Selling Fast — Order Today | > 30% units sold within 72 hrs |
| Editor's Pick | Editor's Pick | Handpicked by our Editors | Curated features / lookbooks |

**Rules**
- Use one badge per product tile; two maximum on PDP.
- Sale badge always wins over discovery badges (Trending, New).
- Never combine "Best Seller" with "New Arrival".
- Title Case, no all-caps except "SALE" on banner strips.
