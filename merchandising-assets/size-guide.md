# Size Guide — Elite Brands

All measurements are in inches unless stated. Garment measurements are flat, not body measurements. Add 1–2" ease for a relaxed fit.

---

## Unstitched

Standard 3-piece unstitched suits ship with generous, uncut yardage.

| Piece | Length | Width |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shirt (front + back) | 2.5 metres | 44" |
| Trouser | 2.5 metres | 44" |
| Dupatta | 2.5 metres | 44"–52" |

Notes:
- Fabric may shrink 2–4% after first wash — pre-wash before stitching.
- Lawn and cambric are typically 2.5 m; luxury and festive collections may include 3.0 m shirt lengths.

## How to Measure Yourself

1. **Bust:** across the fullest part, keeping tape parallel to the floor.
2. **Waist:** at the natural waistline (narrowest point).
3. **Hip:** around the fullest part, ~8" below the waist.
4. **Shirt length:** from shoulder point straight down.
5. **Sleeve:** from shoulder seam to desired cuff.

If between sizes, size up for a comfortable fit.
